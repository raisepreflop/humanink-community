# El calendario de contenidos — estructura

Cuando el calendario no se puede generar como Excel (en el Studio, o sin Python), se entrega con la misma
estructura en tablas, en este orden:

## 1. Weekly Calendar — vista semanal

Una tabla por semana. Filas: los canales; columnas: los días.

- Días: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
- Canales (red · formato): Instagram · Reel; Instagram · Carousel/Feed; Instagram · Story; Facebook · Page post; Facebook · Group; YouTube · Video/Short
- Cada celda: la pieza de ese día en ese canal, con su temperatura de embudo (TOFU frío · MOFU templado · BOFU caliente), o vacía.

## 2. Content List — lista detallada de piezas

Una fila por pieza, con estas columnas: Week | Day | Network | Format | Size (px) | Pillar | Temperature | Hook (1st line) | Message / Core idea | CTA | Destination | Hashtags | Publish time | Status | Notes

## 3. KPI Tracker — seguimiento

Una fila por semana y red (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube), con estas columnas: Week | Network | Reach | Engagement Rate % | Saves / Shares | Link clicks | New followers | Conv. Substack | Conv. WhatsApp | Conv. Tally | Attributable sales | Notes / Key insight

## 4. Hook Bank — banco de ganchos (estructura gancho-mensaje-CTA)

| Hook type | Hook (first 2 lines) | Emotion it triggers | Best format | Temperature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Uncomfortable question | Why do most writers never finish their novel? / (And how you can be the exception) | Curiosity + identity | Reel / Carousel cover | TOFU |
| Controversial statement | 80% of writing advice is garbage. / Here's what actually works. | Outrage + curiosity | Reel | TOFU |
| Personal story | 12 publishers rejected me. / Today I have [N] published books. | Empathy + hope | Reel / FB Post | TOFU-MOFU |
| Surprising fact | The average reader decides in 7 seconds whether to buy a book. / This influences that decision. | Surprise + urgency | Carousel | TOFU |
| Counterintuitive | Writing faster makes your novels better. / Not the other way around. | Curiosity + bewilderment | Reel | TOFU |
| Mistake revelation | I made the mistake every new writer makes. / And I caught it on page 300. | Empathy + learning | FB Post / Reel | MOFU |
| Numbered list | 5 books that changed how I write. / (No one mentions the 3rd) | FOMO + curiosity | Carousel | TOFU |
| Testimonial | "I read your [N] books in one week." / Someone told me this this morning. | Social proof + emotion | Story / Post | MOFU-BOFU |
| Behind the scenes | This is my desk at 6am. / And what I have on screen right now. | Intimacy + connection | Story / Reel | MOFU |
| Direct CTA | My new book is now available. / You can read the first chapter free here → 👇 | Urgency + opportunity | Story / Post | BOFU |

Adapta los ganchos al libro y al género: son el modelo, no el contenido.
